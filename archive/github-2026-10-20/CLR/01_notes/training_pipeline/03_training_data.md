# CLR Training Data

## Three-Layer Design (60/30/10)
- 60% PersonaChat — neutral continuity
- 30% ESConv — emotional trajectory
- 10% Bluemoon — dynamic range (capped, filtered)

## GSM8K Multi-turn Data
- 4218 train / 463 test trajectories
- Each trajectory = 2-5 reasoning turns
- S_t is the only channel between turns
